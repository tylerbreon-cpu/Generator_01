Step 2 — Choose the 7 Pillars

Pillar 1 has been split into Player Fantasy and Growth Pattern. Every pillar includes a manual override field.

Pillar 1

What the player feels like moment to moment.

Pillar 2

How the fantasy escalates over the match, story, or mastery curve.

Pillar 3

The broad outcome they create in the match. This is not a class label.

Pillar 4

The memorable gameplay hook. Broad enough to support many ability kits.

Pillar 5

Motivation fused with flaw. This creates built-in character tension.

Pillar 6

How the character relates to allies, banter, squad identity, and emotional texture.

Pillar 7

Visual motif plus source of power. This should imply shape language, VFX, lore, and ability logic.
