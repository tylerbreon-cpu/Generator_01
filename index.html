Step 2 — Choose the 7 Pillars

Choose seven pillars. Each dropdown is now separated by design layer: hero fantasy, growth, tactical job, signature mechanic, motivation/flaw, team dynamic, and visual/power identity.

Pillar 1

The concrete character identity that expresses the player motivation: what the hero feels like to embody.

Pillar 2

How the fantasy escalates over the match, story, or mastery curve.

Pillar 3

The tactical output they create in the match. This should describe what they do, not who they are.

Pillar 4

The memorable gameplay hook. Broad enough to support many ability kits.

Pillar 5

Motivation fused with flaw. This creates built-in character tension.

Pillar 6

How the character relates to allies, banter, squad identity, and emotional texture.

Pillar 7

Visual motif plus source of power. This should imply silhouette, VFX, lore, and ability logic.
