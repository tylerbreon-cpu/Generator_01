Run Alignment Pass

These controls adjust earlier dropdowns based on the selected archetype, alignment mode, and tension source. They are intentionally opinionated, but you can always override the results manually.

Recommended workflow: choose or randomize a Primary Archetype → click Apply Alignment Mode → click Apply Tension Source if the concept feels too predictable → Generate GPT Text.
Rule of thumb: keep the player promise obvious, then make the person or presentation surprising. The player should instantly understand what they do, but still want to know who they are.
